How Much Does a Photo Booth Rental Cost in Los Angeles?

If you're planning a wedding, corporate event, or birthday party in Southern California, one of the first questions you'll ask is: how much does a photo booth rental cost in Los Angeles? The short answer is anywhere from $600 to $1,050 or more, depending on the package, hours, and add-ons you choose. Below we break down real Evergrain pricing — no vague ranges, no hidden fees — so you can budget with confidence.

What Drives the Cost of a Photo Booth Rental?

Understanding the factors behind pricing helps you get the most value from your budget. Here are the four main variables:

Rental Hours

Every package has a minimum rental period — 3 hours for The Candid and The Moment, 4 hours for The Glam. Most weddings and galas run 4–5 hours of booth time; a shorter birthday party or shower may only need the 3-hour minimum. Adding hours beyond the minimum is the single biggest way the final price moves.

Prints and Photo Formats

All packages include 2×6 photo strips printed on-site. The Moment and The Glam add a 4×6 option as well. If you want unlimited on-site prints for every guest, guestbook or scrapbook service, or specialty formats, those are available as add-ons that adjust the total. Digital sharing — text, email, AirDrop, QR — is unlimited and included in every tier.

Travel Distance

Travel within 30 miles of Hollywood is included in every package at no extra charge. That covers most of Los Angeles — from Santa Monica to Pasadena, Downtown to Burbank. For venues beyond that radius, such as parts of Ventura County or the Inland Empire, we quote a transparent travel fee before you book so there are no surprises on event day.

Add-Ons and Customization

Custom backdrops, branded overlays, GIFs and boomerangs, a red carpet setup, and additional print formats are all available. Add-ons are priced individually so you can layer in exactly what your event needs and nothing it doesn't. Browse the full list on our add-ons page.

Why Equipment Quality Affects Price

One reason photo booth rental prices vary so widely in Los Angeles is the gap between booth types. iPad-based booths use a tablet camera, which struggles in dim lighting and produces soft, low-resolution images. A professional open-air DSLR booth — the kind Evergrain uses for every event — captures sharp, vibrant photos with studio-grade lighting. The difference is obvious in the prints and especially in larger 4×6 formats. When you compare quotes, ask what camera the company uses. The answer tells you a lot about the final photo quality your guests will take home.

What to Expect on Event Day

Setup and teardown are included in every package and happen outside your rental hours — we arrive early and leave after the last guest. Your on-site attendant manages the booth, assists guests, keeps the props organized, and ensures the printer stays loaded. You don't need to assign a coordinator or volunteer to babysit the booth.

Within 48 hours of the event, we deliver a full digital gallery to your inbox with every photo from the night.

Tips for Getting the Best Value

  • Book early. We take one event per day. Popular weekends in wedding season (May–October) and holiday party season (November–December) fill months ahead.
  • Match the tier to the event. A backyard birthday doesn't need The Glam; a 200-guest wedding might. Start with the tier that fits your guest count and level of customization.
  • Bundle strategically. If you know you want prints, a custom backdrop, and a guestbook, ask about those during your initial quote — we can walk you through the most cost-effective combination.
  • Ask about the travel zone. If your venue is near the 30-mile boundary, confirming whether it's inside or outside avoids a last-minute surprise on the invoice.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a photo booth rental cost in Los Angeles?

Photo booth rental in Los Angeles typically ranges from $600 to $1,050 or more depending on the package, rental hours, and add-ons. At Evergrain, The Candid starts at $600 for a 3-hour minimum, The Moment starts at $750 for 3 hours, and The Glam starts at $1,050 for a 4-hour minimum.

Is there a travel fee for photo booth rental in Los Angeles?

At Evergrain, travel within 30 miles of Hollywood is included in every package at no extra charge. For venues beyond that radius — such as parts of Ventura County or the Inland Empire — we quote a transparent travel fee before you book.

What is the difference between an open-air DSLR booth and an iPad booth?

An open-air DSLR booth uses a professional camera with studio-grade lighting, producing sharp, high-resolution photos. iPad booths use tablet cameras, which struggle in low light and produce noticeably softer images. Evergrain uses a DSLR with intentional lighting for every event.

Are prints included in a photo booth rental?

All three Evergrain packages include 2×6 photo strip prints and unlimited digital sharing via text, email, AirDrop, and QR code. The Moment and The Glam also offer a 4×6 print option. Additional print formats are available as add-ons.

How far in advance should I book a photo booth in Los Angeles?

We recommend booking as soon as you have a confirmed date. Evergrain takes only one event per day, so popular weekends — especially during wedding season (May through October) and holiday party season (November through December) — fill months in advance.
