Why Los Angeles chooses Evergrain

A photo booth that earns its place at every LA event.

Los Angeles sets a higher bar for everything — and your photo booth rental should clear it. Evergrain was founded in LA because we saw too many events settle for dim iPad booths and flimsy curtains that clashed with the rest of the design. Our open-air DSLR booth was designed from the ground up for Southern California light and Southern California expectations: real camera, real prints, real attention to how the setup looks inside your venue.

We shoot on a Canon DSLR with studio strobes dialed to the room. The result is print-quality images with accurate skin tones and crisp detail — not the washed-out snapshots most photo booths produce. Every session includes a custom template designed around your event's palette, fonts, and branding, so the prints feel like a curated piece of the evening rather than a novelty afterthought.

Whether you're hosting 40 guests at an intimate dinner in Silver Lake or 300 at a corporate gala downtown, the booth scales without compromise. We arrive 60 to 90 minutes early, handle every cable and backdrop, host the booth through the last photo, and break down without a trace. You never touch a prop bin or troubleshoot a printer.

Frequently asked questions
How much does a photo booth rental cost in Los Angeles?

Evergrain packages start at $600 for The Candid (3-hour minimum), $750 for The Moment (3-hour minimum), and $1,050 for The Glam (4-hour minimum). Every package includes the open-air DSLR booth, custom templates, premium backdrops and props, an on-site attendant, and setup and breakdown.

Is there a travel fee for Los Angeles events?

Travel within 30 miles of Hollywood is included in every package. Beyond that radius, we quote a transparent mileage-based fee before you book — no surprises on your invoice.

How far in advance should I book a photo booth in LA?

We take one event per day to give every client our full attention. Weekend dates in peak season (May through October) fill months ahead, so we recommend reserving as soon as your venue is confirmed.

What is included in an Evergrain photo booth package?

Every package includes the open-air DSLR booth, a custom-designed photo template, a premium backdrop, curated props, an on-site attendant for the full duration, and complimentary setup and breakdown. Prints and a digital gallery are standard.

Do you bring your own backdrop and lighting?

Yes. We arrive with a full setup — backdrop, studio lighting, and all equipment. We only need a 6-by-8-foot footprint and a standard power outlet. Our team handles setup 60 to 90 minutes before your start time.

Can I customize the photo template for my event?

Absolutely. We design a custom template that matches your event's theme, colors, and branding. You approve the design before the event, and every print and digital copy uses it.
